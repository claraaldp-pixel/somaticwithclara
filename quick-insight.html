01

Common questions.

Do I need to know anything about Human Design?

No. You can arrive with absolutely nothing more than a question or a feeling that there may be a different way to understand what is happening. I will meet you there.

Can you tell me what decision to make?

I will not make a decision for you, but we can look at what is making it hard to hear yourself. The goal is more clarity and self-trust, not outsourcing your life to a chart.

Is Quick Insight a full chart reading?

No. It is deliberately focused: one question, one personalised perspective, and one practical experiment. If you are looking for ongoing support, the 1:1 containers may be a better fit.

What can I ask about?

A repeating pattern, a decision, work, relationships, energy, or the gap between the life that looks fine and the one that feels like yours. People often ask about not being able to hear their Sacral, Projector exhaustion and recognition, getting stuck before they can initiate, or losing momentum. I do not answer medical, legal, financial, crisis, or predictive-certainty questions.
