More is happening
than you can see.

Days can look almost identical from the outside. Wake up. Work. Scroll a bit. Remember the washing in the machine six hours later. Repeat.

But a lot is happening internally, often below your conscious awareness. Your needs change. An old fear gets louder. A desire you had pushed aside quietly returns. Inner Sky gives you regular space to notice those movements before they vanish under the next busy week.

Powered by the lunar cycle

Your awareness and where you place your attention are two of the most precious things you have. 🤍

When life is on autopilot

Another month passes and you cannot say what changed.

  • What matters to you keeps becoming something you will return to later.
  • You notice your needs only when they become impossible to ignore.
  • Progress counts only when it is obvious, public or productive.
  • You ask everyone else what they think before you ask yourself.

With a regular place to notice

You can see your inner life while it is happening.

  • You name what deserves your attention now.
  • You catch subtle shifts before your mind dismisses them.
  • You can recognise progress that no one else can see yet.
  • You make your next choice with more contact with yourself.

A few useful answers.

Before your brain opens another tab.

What if I cannot attend live?

You can watch the replay and use the reflection in your own time. The live gathering gives us a shared beginning, but missing it does not mean you have missed the whole cycle.

What if I join and then barely post?

You are still welcome. Share, respond quietly or simply read. You do not need to become a perfect community person who comments before breakfast.

Do I need to know Human Design or astrology?

No. Human Design discussions are optional context, and I explain the parts we use. The somatic practices and reflections stand on their own. You do not need to know your gates, houses or why everyone keeps talking about Mercury.

What if this becomes another membership I forget to use?

Inner Sky follows one recurring lunar rhythm instead of asking you to keep up with daily lessons. You can return at the New Moon, check in midway or use the Full Moon practice. The library is there when you need it, not as homework.

What if it is not what I expected?

Email me within seven days of joining and I will refund your first payment. I would appreciate hearing what did not fit, but you do not need to write a dissertation to qualify.

How does the introductory price work?

Join now at £7 per month, charged as $9 USD by Skool, and your price is locked in for the life of your active membership. I will review the price after three months for new members only.

Is this coaching or therapy?

Inner Sky is a community space for reflection, education and somatic practice. It is not individual coaching, therapy, medical advice, crisis support or a substitute for qualified mental health care.

You do not have to know exactly what you are changing. Begin by making enough room to notice yourself.
