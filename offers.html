Common questions

What is included in the Human Design Report?

A nine-section digital report built specifically for your chart, not a generic type description. It covers your type, strategy and inner authority; your Definition (how your energy is structured); an Energy Map of every defined and undefined centre with what each one means for you; the thread of patterns running through your channels; your Sun Gate; and body-based practices for each layer of your chart.

The Nervous System section maps each survival state, sympathetic activation, functional freeze, dorsal collapse, and fawn, to how they show up in your specific design: the body signals, what's happening beneath the behaviour, and what helps. Delivered by email within 5–7 days.

Do I need a discovery call before booking somatic sessions?

Yes. A free 30-minute discovery call is required before booking. It's a conversation to find out whether we're a good fit and what the right entry point is for where you are now.

Can I book a single session or do I need a package?

Both are available. A single somatic session is £90; a package of four is £320. A discovery call is required before booking either.

What does a somatic session involve?

Somatic sessions are body-based 1:1 coaching. The work starts from where you actually are — not where you think you should be — using somatic practices to process and shift nervous system patterns.
