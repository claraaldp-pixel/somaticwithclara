Meet Somatic Pause.

A private app for regular check-ins between sessions. You can notice the patterns that keep showing up, learn what they feel like in your body, take notes, and choose a practice that brings you back to the present.

Your life does not have to
look good only on paper.

The point is not to make it prettier for Instagram. The point is to feel more alive inside it.

Bring the difficult conversation, the decision you keep delaying, or the feeling that your life belongs to everyone except you. We start with what is true that day.

Some sessions are quiet. Some are emotional. Some end with a small thing to try during the week that feels almost too simple to matter. Those are often the ones that stay with you.

I will help you slow down enough to notice what you need, without talking yourself out of it five seconds later. We will work with the body, the stories you have inherited, and the hopes you may have put on a shelf for later.

Sessions are available in English, French, and Spanish.

What I will
not do.

I cannot live your life for you, and I will not pretend I know better than you do. I can help you hear yourself beneath the noise, believe in what you find, and take the next step without leaving yourself behind.

Common questions.

"I have tried everything."

You probably do not need more things to try. We will slow down and see what is actually happening, then find a response that feels like it belongs to you.

"I do not know what is wrong."

Perfect. Well, not perfect for you, obviously. But you do not need a tidy story before you start. A feeling, a question, or the sense that you have lost yourself somewhere along the way is plenty.

Do I need to know Human Design?

No. I may bring it in when it gives us a useful angle, but it does not predict your future or tell you who you should be.

Is this therapy?

No. This is coaching and education focused on somatic awareness, emotional integration, and self-trust. It does not replace therapy, medical care, crisis support, or qualified mental-health treatment.
