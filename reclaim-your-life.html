Questions

Things you might
be wondering.

I don't have trauma. Is this still for me?
+
Yes. You don't need a diagnosis or a story that qualifies. If something feels quietly off, if you're functioning but not quite in it, that's enough. The nervous system doesn't distinguish between "big" events and chronic low-grade pressure. Both leave patterns. You don't need to justify being here.
I've tried somatic work before. Will this be different?
+
Possibly. Most approaches give you tools without helping you understand which tool, when, and why. The quiz names your specific survival pattern. The Somatic Pause app gives you practices matched to your state right now, not what helps in general. That specificity changes things.
How much time does this take?
+
As much or as little as you have. The quiz takes five minutes. The library is designed for short sessions. The Somatic Pause app is for whenever you need it. The whole structure is built around your pace, not a schedule you have to keep up with.
Is this therapy?
+
No. This is education, somatic practice tools, and community. It doesn't replace clinical mental health support. It offers a way to understand your nervous system patterns and work with them. Not just cope around them.
