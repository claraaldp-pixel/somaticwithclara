How we work.

We begin with what is happening in your life now. These three lenses help us understand a pattern and practise a different response.

Identity and Self Concept

Notice the stories and expectations that shape how you see yourself and what you allow yourself to want.

Nervous System Capacity

I’m Clara, a somatic coach and Human Design guide. In our sessions, we pay attention to what you feel as well as what you think. I help you notice the patterns that make your own wants hard to hear, then find ways to respond that fit your real life.

The point is to feel more present and at home in your life, not to make it look better from the outside.

The truth bit

I am not here to give you answers.

You are the expert on your life. I can help you notice the stress responses, stories and inherited expectations that make your own voice harder to hear. Then we practise listening without immediately talking yourself out of what you know. But I will never tell you what you should do. Only you know.

This is coaching and education, not therapy, medical care, crisis support, or a substitute for qualified mental-health treatment. Human Design is used as a reflective framework, not for prediction, diagnosis, or making your decisions for you.
